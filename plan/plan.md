# Come In App Restoration and Completion

## What it is, in two lines
Restore the uploaded Come In project from its backup and resolve the visible conflict or incomplete state.
Deliver a polished, usable version of the existing product without changing its core purpose.

## Who it's for
People who need the Come In app’s primary workflow to be clear, reliable, and easy to complete.

## Core features and experience
- Preserve the existing branding, content, navigation, and product intent wherever they are already clear.
- Make the main user journey work from the first screen through its completion state.
- Resolve conflicting or broken screens so the experience feels consistent rather than partially merged.
- Keep important actions obvious, with clear feedback for loading, success, empty, and error states.
- Ensure the experience is comfortable on both desktop and mobile screen sizes.

## Phase 1: what gets built now
- Reconstruct the application from the uploaded backup.
- Identify and resolve the conflict state that prevents the app from feeling complete.
- Finish the highest-priority user flow end to end.
- Polish the primary screens, navigation, forms, actions, and feedback states while retaining the existing product direction.
- Leave secondary enhancements outside Phase 1 unless they are required for the primary flow to work.
